I work across the whole product - PostgreSQL schema and NestJS services on one end, Next.js
interfaces on the other.

Over the past year I've shipped eight production products as one of the core developers at
**Foxycode**: an intercity bus ticketing platform, a social-media automation SaaS, a US-market
cashback platform, a football analytics service and a tender-processing CRM.

One of those backends I moved off Strapi onto NestJS with **zero downtime** - data migrated,
traffic cut over, users kept booking tickets the whole time.

**What I usually get handed:** booking and payment flows · payout and cashback engines · admin,
carrier and dispatcher back-offices · third-party integrations (Instagram, TikTok, Telegram,
Prozorro, Google/Apple OAuth) · CMS migrations · CI/CD and deployments · performance rescue work
(Lighthouse 50-60 => 85-100)

> Most of my commercial code lives in private repositories under client organizations.
> Happy to walk through architecture decisions on a call.
